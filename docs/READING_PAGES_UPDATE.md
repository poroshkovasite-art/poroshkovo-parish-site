# Reading pages update · 2 November 2026

The Catechesis page now presents three independent resources: the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, youth YOUCAT and YOUCAT for Kids. The Compendium uses the owner's Ukrainian description verbatim and links directly to the Odessa Exarchate's original PDF in a new tab. The English card explicitly identifies the book's language as Ukrainian. No Compendium PDF or full book text is stored in the repository or production output. The six existing children's activities and their source links remain intact.

The Divine Liturgy follows the owner's Word edition. All 699 reading blocks, the diagram and three native footnotes are represented in order; the diagram is one of those reading blocks. Inline roles, red italic rubrics, emphasis, stresses, alternatives and repetitions are retained. The previous scan edition is archived without silently merging its wording with Word. See [the edition comparison](LITURGY_WORD_COMPARISON.md).

The reading page uses a continuous column, 22 px serif text, line height 1.45 and short paragraph spacing. Times New Roman leads the body font stack because the installed Georgia gives combining Ukrainian stresses a visible advance width; no proprietary font files are redistributed. Text controls offer 18–32 px, reading mode, a collapsed contents list, footnote return links, back-to-top and A4 printing. The English route points to the Ukrainian text. Public scan links, scan images and the scan PDF are excluded from the production build; the original files and provenance remain in Git and the local backup.

Validation: production build and structural checks passed for 38 bilingual routes and 2,520 internal references. The Word integrity and browser checks passed at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, including both languages, 702 displayed Word blocks, all footnote/contents targets, largest-font overflow, reading controls, print visibility and the Compendium popup URL. Eight axe checks reported no WCAG A/AA violations. Before/after screenshots are retained in the local audit and backup. The first 30 liturgical speeches require 54–64% less vertical space than the previous page, depending on screen width.

Reproduction: build production, serve it on port 4186, then run `node scripts/liturgy-book-qa.mjs`. `BOOK_BASE_URL` can point at a deployed site. The source importer accepts the owner's DOCX and `--scan-source` pointing to the original pre-Word JSON; it refuses to compare the Word edition against itself. The Word source is retained in the private working archive and backup, outside the public build.

Old public scan-image and scan-PDF URLs redirect to the continuous reading page. This also addresses Cloudflare's retention of previously cached assets after a deployment. The files remain in the source archive.

The three symbolic History panels now center their icon, date and caption as a single composition. The Zakarpattia panel no longer inherits photo-only styling that removed its padding. Dates remain 1646, 1381 and 1751 · 1949 · 2002; text and photo panels retain their existing arrangement. UA/EN passed ten layout checks across the same five widths and four axe checks. Screenshots are retained under `audit/history-alignment` in the backup.

Publication verification and the exact commit/deployment identifiers are recorded in the local release report after deployment.
